Fifty years at the same bench

五十年同一張裁床

A suit is three hundred decisions. We make them slowly, once, and get them right.

Master Chan opened the shop in 1974 and still cuts every pattern himself. Cloth is sourced from English and Italian mills, linings sewn by hand, buttonholes finished one at a time.

陳師傅一九七四年開舖,至今仍親自開每一個紙樣。布料採自英國及意大利布廠,裡布手工縫製,鈕門逐個鎖。
