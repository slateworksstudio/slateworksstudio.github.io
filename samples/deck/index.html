Harbour Peak Roasters

Specialty coffee, roasted in Wong Chuk Hang, delivered across Hong Kong Island within 24 hours.

Seed round · September 2026 · confidential · 種子輪融資簡介

The problem

Hong Kong drinks great coffee. It just isn't fresh.

  • Most specialty beans sold in HK are roasted overseas and sit 4-8 weeks in transit and warehousing.
  • Flavour peaks 7-21 days after roasting - by the time beans reach a HK kitchen, the best is gone.
  • Local cafés want local supply chains; consumers increasingly do too.
The solution

Roast-to-door in 24 hours, Island-wide

  • Micro-roastery in Wong Chuk Hang roasting five days a week.
  • Order by 6pm, beans at your door - or your café - by 6pm tomorrow.
  • Subscription model: weekly, fortnightly or monthly, pause anytime.

Business model

Subscriptions carry it. Wholesale scales it.

  • Direct subscriptions: HK$168/month average, 62% gross margin.
  • Wholesale to cafés: HK$95/kg, 41% margin, weekly standing orders.
  • Corporate gifting: seasonal spikes, highest margin (68%).
The ask

HK$3.5M seed to double roasting capacity

  • 60% - second roaster, packaging line, cold-chain delivery fleet.
  • 25% - Kowloon and New Territories expansion, wholesale sales hire.
  • 15% - working capital and green-bean forward contracts.

Target close: Q1 2027.
