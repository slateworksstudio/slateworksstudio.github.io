Three quick wins for humid-weather hair

  • Switch to a sulphate-free wash - your colour lasts weeks longer.
  • Apply serum to damp hair, never dry. It seals the cuticle instead of sitting on top.
  • Sleep on silk (or tie a loose braid). Friction is the quiet killer of shine.
