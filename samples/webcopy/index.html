Homepage hero

The first good decision of your day.
Specialty coffee on Third Street, roasted to order and poured by people who remember your name. Open 7:30, seven days. Why it works: a promise, a place, a personality - in three lines. Sub-headline carries opening hours so mobile visitors never hunt for them.

About page, opening paragraph

First Light started with a stubborn idea: that a neighbourhood deserves coffee as good as Central's, without the Central queue. We roast small, pour carefully, and keep the chairs comfortable enough that nobody rushes you. Sai Ying Pun raised us; the regulars keep us honest. Why it works: origin, values and community in four sentences, with a local anchor ("Sai Ying Pun") that search engines and neighbours both reward.
First Light 由一個執念開始:社區都值得有靚咖啡,唔使去中環排隊。小批量烘焙,用心沖泡,櫈坐到舒服。西營盤養大我哋,熟客令我哋唔敢鬆懈。

Google Business Profile description (750 chars)

First Light Coffee is a specialty coffee shop on Third Street, Sai Ying Pun, Hong Kong. We roast single-origin beans in small batches and serve espresso drinks, filter coffee and fresh pastries daily from 7:30am. Dine in, takeaway, or order beans to brew at home - retail bags and brewing gear in store. Quiet corners for work, big tables for weekends. Dog-friendly outside seating. Walk-ins welcome. Why it works: front-loads category and location for local search, answers the four questions every profile visitor has (what, where, when, can I sit/work/bring the dog).
First Light Coffee 位於西營盤第三街,主打精品咖啡,單品豆小批量烘焙,每日朝早7:30供應特濃咖啡、手沖及新鮮酥點。堂食外賣皆可,店內有零售咖啡豆及沖煮器具。環境安靜適合工作,週末有大檯。戶外座位歡迎狗隻。
